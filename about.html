What attendees get

The workshop is free, and every attendee leaves with continuing access to Exea Labs support.

  • Mentor FeedbackGuidance while students develop research plans and prepare their pitches.
  • Research ResourcesSpecial resources that help promising ideas move forward.
  • CommunityOngoing access to the Exea Labs research community.
  • GPU ComputeCompute access through Exea Labs for every workshop attendee.
