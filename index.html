Who Should Come

This workshop is for Bay Area high school students at every experience level, including students who have never done research before.

  • Choose a strong, feasible topic and avoid ideas that are too broad
  • Turn an interest into a focused question and testable hypothesis
  • Design rigorous research with baselines, controls, variables, and metrics
  • Understand peer review, preprints, conferences, and journals
  • Use Google Scholar, Semantic Scholar, Zotero, Overleaf, GitHub, and public datasets
