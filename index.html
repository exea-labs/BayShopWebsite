Who Should Come

This workshop is for Bay Area high school students who want to start doing real research. No prior experience required.

  • You have no research experience and want to start
  • You don't know how to pick a topic or design a study
  • You want to solidify your research foundation
  • You're looking for research partners and community

The Five Hours

A structured, hands-on afternoon that takes you from zero to a complete research plan.

  1. 12:30

    Welcome

    Examples of student research that worked and what made it successful.

  2. 1:00

    Choosing a Topic

    What makes a research question work, how to do a literature search, then pick yours.

  3. 1:45

    Designing the Study

    Baselines, variables, metrics, and reproducibility.

  4. 2:30

    Getting Published

    Peer review, preprints, conferences, and what arXiv is.

  5. 3:15

    Tools of the Trade

    Google Scholar, Zotero, LaTeX, GitHub, public datasets, and free compute.

  6. 4:00

    Pitches & Awards

    Work time to develop your plan, then 90-second pitches to the judging panel.

How Judging Works

Each team delivers a 90-second pitch to a panel of experienced researchers and industry professionals.

The Panel

Published high school researchers from Exea Labs and professionals with real-world research backgrounds.

Criteria

  • Research Direction — Specific, feasible, and novel
  • Study Design — Sound methods, reproducible
  • Pitch Clarity — Clear and concise delivery
  • Potential Impact — Could this work lead somewhere real?
