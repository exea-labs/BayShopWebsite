Exea Labs

A student-run research collective for high school students. Members get compute, mentorship, and a research community at no cost. Founded April 2026, our goal is to lower barrier of entry for students to push science forward and explore real-world problems.

What members get

All of it free. No paid tiers, no application fee.

  • ComputeAMD accelerators, including a node of eight MI300X GPUs.
  • MentorshipMentors who read drafts and review methodology.
  • CommunitySeveral hundred researchers across 25 countries.
  • CreditsAPI credits and research tools from partner companies.
