Who should come

This workshop is designed to kickstart high school students with little to no research experience. Our goal is to take you from zero to a full research topic and design.

  • No experience
  • Don't know how to start
  • Looking to solidfy your foundation
  • Looking for research partners

Awards

The day ends with 90-second pitches. A panel picks outstanding presenters to recieve additonal awards/prizes. Expect awards for:

  • Best Research DirectionThe most specific, feasible question.
  • Strongest 90-Second PitchThe clearest explanation.
  • Best OverallJudged as the most promising project as a whole.

Sponsor-backed awards may include research tools, credits, and/or cool prizes.

The five hours

  1. WelcomeExamples of student research that worked.
  2. Choosing a topicWhat makes one work, a literature search, then pick yours.
  3. Designing the studyBaselines, variables, metrics, reproducibility.
  4. Getting publishedPeer review, preprints, conferences, and what arXiv is.
  5. ToolsScholar, Zotero, LaTeX, GitHub, datasets, free compute.
  6. Pitches and awardsWork time to develop research plan, then 90-second pitches.
